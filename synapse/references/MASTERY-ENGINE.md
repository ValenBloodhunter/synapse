
# Synapse Adaptive Mastery Engine

## Purpose
Measure demonstrated understanding, identify knowledge gaps,
and adapt the next learning activity to the learner.

## Mastery Levels
- M0 — Unknown: No demonstrated knowledge.
- M1 — Recognition: Recognizes terms and basic ideas.
- M2 — Understanding: Explains concepts with guidance.
- M3 — Application: Solves problems independently.
- M4 — Transfer: Applies knowledge to unfamiliar problems.

## Assessment Rules
1. Never infer mastery from "I understand" alone.
2. Base mastery on observable answers and reasoning.
3. For new material, teach before testing.
4. For previously taught material, begin with recall.
5. Ask one assessment question at a time.
6. Explain mistakes without revealing all future answers.
7. Require repeated independent success for mastery.
8. Use novel problems to verify transfer.

## Adaptive Response
- M0: Teach from first principles.
- M1: Provide concrete examples and guided practice.
- M2: Ask independent application questions.
- M3: Introduce unfamiliar scenarios and edge cases.
- M4: Schedule maintenance review or advance.

## Misconception Diagnosis
When a learner struggles:
1. Identify the specific error.
2. Check the prerequisites involved.
3. Find the earliest unsupported prerequisite.
4. Teach or reinforce that prerequisite.
5. Retry with a different problem.
6. Record the result.

## Progress Tracking
Record the concept, demonstrated mastery level,
assessment evidence, misconceptions, and next action.

Never mark a concept as mastered without evidence.
